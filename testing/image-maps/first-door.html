Option 1

The heavy wood groans as you turn the cold, porcelain knob. Pushing the door open, a rush of crisp, pine-scented air sweeps over you. The dark, cramped cabin completely vanishes.Instead, you find yourself standing at the edge of a sunlit meadow carpeted in silver-blue wildflowers. In the distance, a mountain peak mirrors the sky, and a crystal-clear stream bubbles right past your boots. Resting on a smooth stone next to the water is a leather-bound journal with a fresh ink quill—waiting for you to write your own next chapter.
