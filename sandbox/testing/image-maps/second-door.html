Option 2

Your fingers wrap around the rough, wrought-iron handle. It clicks loudly, and as you give it a firm push, a warm, golden glow spills across your face. The smell of rich cinnamon, melted sugar, and old paper fills the room.You step forward into a massive, hidden library. A massive stone fireplace crackles softly in the corner, flanked by two oversized velvet armchairs. Towering bookshelves stretch all the way up to a stained-glass ceiling, packed with thousands of glowing, ancient books. On a small side table, a fresh mug of hot cocoa steam billows gently, inviting you to stay a while.
